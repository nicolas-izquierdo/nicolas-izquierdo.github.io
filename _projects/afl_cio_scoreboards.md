The AFL-CIO publishes annual Legislative Scoreboards rating every member of the U.S.
Congress on their votes for working families. This dataset compiles the original
scoreboards from **1967 to 2025** for both chambers and provides machine-readable,
bill-level CSV files for use in quantitative research on labor politics, legislative
behavior, and political representation.

**Coverage:** U.S. House of Representatives and Senate, 1967–2025 · one CSV per chamber
and year, chamber-level totals, and the original scoreboard PDFs.
