The AFL-CIO publishes annual Legislative Scorecards rating every member of the U.S.
House of Representatives on their votes for working families. This dataset compiles
and standardizes those ratings from **1980 to 2025**, linking them to member
characteristics (party, state, district) for use in quantitative research on labor
politics, legislative behavior, and political representation.

**Coverage:** U.S. House of Representatives, 97th–118th Congresses (1980–2025).
