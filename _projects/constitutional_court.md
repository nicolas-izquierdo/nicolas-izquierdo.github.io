Spain's Constitutional Court (*Tribunal Constitucional*) is the supreme interpreter of the
Spanish Constitution. This dataset compiles and standardizes all *amparo* rulings issued by
the Court from **1980 to 2025**, covering 7,434 decisions. It includes structured case-level
data on parties, constitutional rights invoked, outcomes, and dissenting opinions, together
with each ruling's full text.

**Coverage:** Spanish Constitutional Court, 1980–2025 · 7,434 *amparo* rulings.
